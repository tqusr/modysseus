Fine tuning

12°/min
Gyro drift while parked. We now estimate the bias whenever the wheels say stopped.
2.34×
Skid-steer scrub. The effective wheel separation is 2.34 times the geometric one.
201 counts
For a 2.00 m drive. The encoder “metres” were really centimetre counts.
29.5 MB
Per camera cloud. It starved the collision monitor until we downsampled.
